  1. Gotta Catch 'Em All

    by Max

    Be the very best like no one ever was in a game of Clocktower set in Kanto. This script includes a selection of the original 151 Pokemon (one of each type) and some of the villains encountered in the original anime and games, with nods to Pokemon the First Movie.

  2. In this edition of Blood on the Clocktower, a few keywords are changed to fit the gentler and less adult theme. Abilities use these modified keywords, but tools for the Storyteller such as night reminders and the How To Run features of the almanac still refer to these keywords by their original names to reduce confusion during the game.

    Trainer.....Player
    Grunts.....Minions
    Opponent.....Demon
    Villains...Evil Characters
    Pokemon.....Good Characters †
    Knocked out....Executed
    Faint....Die
    Full HP....Living
    Paralyzed.....Drunk
    Revived....Ressurected

    † It's important to note that all good characters are grouped together as "Pokemon." No abilities are affected by this wording, but don't forget that Outsiders and Townsfolk alike are considered Pokemon, while villains like Meowth, Weezing and Mewtwo are NOT.

  3. Onix

    You start knowing a secret phrase. The first time another player says it, they learn who you are that night*.

    “095 Rock Type - As it grows, the stone portions of its body harden to become similar to a diamond, but colored black.”

    Onix uses Rock Polish to show its role to whichever player says its secret phrase.

    Examples

    • Felicia is Onix, and her phrase is "Are tacos sandwiches?" George says the phrase in public, and that night the storytellers wake him and tell him Felicia is Onix.

    How to Run

    Each night after the first, if you heard a player say Onix's phrase, wake them and and point to the player who is Onix, then show them the character token.

    Townsfolk

  4. Dragonite

    Up to three players start knowing you're in play. At least one is good.

    “149 Dragon Type - An extremely rarely seen marine Pokemon. Its intelligence is said to match that of humans.”

    Dragonite's Dragon Dance ability shows them to three different players, and they know at least one of them is good.

    Examples

    • On Night 1, Pikachu, Mewtwo and Meowth wake to learn there's a Dragonite in play.

    • On Night 1, Giovanni, Gengar and Machamp wake to learn there's a Dragonite in play.

    • On Night 1, Machamp and Persian wake to learn there's a Dragonite in play.

    How to Run

    On the first night, wake up to three players (at least one good). Mark those players with Dragon Dance tokens, and show them the Dragonite token.

    Townsfolk

  5. Charizard

    Each night, choose a player. At the end of N3, grunts you’ve chosen faint.

    “006 Fire Type - Spits fire that is hot enough to melt boulders. Known to cause forest fires unintentionally.”

    Charizard uses Fire Spin, an attack that gets more powerful as the nights go on, until any minions caught up in it are executed.

    Examples

    • Dan is Charizard. On night one, he chooses Tom, on night two Steve and on night three Bob. Bob is Meowth, so he dies on night three.

    • Chris is Charizard. On night one, they choose Jo, on night two George and on night three Sal. None of these players are minions, so none of them die.

    • Charizard chooses Vincent, Bray and Hal. Vincent and Bray are Clones that think they're Jynx but are not, so they both die.

    How to Run

    Wake Charizard each night and ask them to point to a player. Mark players they point to with the "Fire Spin" token. At the end of night three, if any players marked with a Fire Spin are minions, mark them as dead.

    Townsfolk

  6. Jynx

    Each night*, if you were nominated today, choose a Pokemon or villain. If they nominated you, they faint tomorrow.

    “124 Ice Type - It seductively wiggles its hips as it walks. It can cause people to dance in unison with it.”

    Jynx uses Reflect to kill the player that tried to get them executed . . . as long as they choose the right character.

    Examples

    • Carlos is Jynx, and Malcolm is Onix. Malcolm nominates Carlos, and that night Carlos chooses Onix. Sometime before dusk tomorrow, Malcolm will die.

    • Aliana is Jynx and Victor is Machamp. Yesterday Victor nominated Aliana, and last night she chose Mewtwo. Victor lives through the day.

    • Fred is Jynx and Mallory is Jessie who chose to register as Pikachu. Last night, Fred picked Pikachu because Mallory nominated him earlier that day. First thing the next morning, the Storyteller announces that Mallory dies.

    How to Run

    Each night after the first, wake Jynx and have them point to a character on the script. If that character nominated Jynx today, at some point tomorrow, announce that player has died.

    Townsfolk

  7. Machamp

    Each night, choose two players. If they’re both Pokemon, you don't faint if they nominate and knock you out today.

    “068 Fighting Type - It uses its four powerful arms to pin the limbs of its foe, then throws the victim over the horizon.”

    Machamp can use Submission to protect themselves from good players' nominations.

    Examples

    • Mark is Machamp, Alan is Alakazam and Sara is Squirtle. Mark chooses Alan and Sara. The next day, Alan nominates Mark. Mark is executed but lives.

    • Horatio is Machamp and Tiffany is Giovanni. Horatio chooses himself and Tiffany. The next day, Tiffany nominates Horatio and he is executed. He dies.

    How to Run

    Each night, wake Machamp and have them point to two players. If they're both good, mark them and Machamp with the Submission tokens. Tomorrow, if either marked player nominates Machamp and they're executed, announce that they don't die.

    Townsfolk

  8. Diglett

    You start by choosing to Dig or use Ancient Power. If you dig, you can't be knocked out. If you use Ancient Power, each night*, learn if you nominated the Opponent.

    “050 Ground Type - It prefers dark places. It spends most of its time underground, though it may pop up in caves.”

    Diglett uses Dig to become safe from execution or uses Ancient Power and potentially learns who the demon is.

    Examples

    • Tish is Diglett and they choose to Dig. They're executed Day 1 and Day 2. They don't die either day.

    • Mordecai is Diglett, and he chooses to use Ancient Power. On Day 2, he nominates the demon, and that night he wakes and learns yes.

    How to Run

    Wake Diglett and have them point to the word Dig or Ancient Power on the script. If they choose Dig, mark them with the Dig token. If they're ever executed, announce that they don't die. If they choose Ancient Power, mark a demon with the Ancient Power token.

    Townsfolk

  9. Magneton

    Each night*, a different trainer learns a true fact about the game. [One Pokemon is a villain.]

    “082 Steel Type - Generates strange radio signals. It raises the temperature by 3.6F degrees within 3,300 feet.”

    Magneton makes one good player evil, but in exchange, their Light Screen reveals a true fact to a different player each night.

    Examples

    • Xander is Magneton and Nair is Squirtle. On Night 5, the Storyteller wakes Nair and tells him there is at least one dead evil player.

    • Bocephus is Magneton and Kale is Weezing. On Night 3, the Storyteller wakes Kale and tells her that one once-per-game ability has been used.

    How to Run

    On the first night of the game, wake a Pokemon player and tell them they're evil. It can be an outsider or a townsfolk. Each night after the first, wake a player that isn't marked with the Light Screen token, tell them a true fact about the game, and mark them with the Light Screen token.

    Townsfolk

  10. Pidgeotto

    Once per game, you may choose a trainer to become safe from villains for 3 Nights.

    “017 Flying Type - Very protective of its sprawling territorial area, this Pokemon will fiercely peck at any intruder.”

    Pidgeotto uses Fly and protects one player from evil for up to three nights.

    Examples

    • Gregor is Pidgeotto, and Mal is Onix. Gregor chose Mal on Night 1, and on Night 2 Giovanni chooses Mal. Mal survives Day 2 and Night 3 to wake up on Day 3, alive.

    • Maddie is Pidgeotto, Samwise is Pikachu and Donnie was the Meowth that chose to become the evil Persian on Night 3. The following day, when Donnie is 'mad' that they're Persian, Pikachu is not executed because Maddie chose Samwise on Night 2.

    • Cora is Pidgeotto and Tally is a Clone Gengar. Cora chooses Tally on Night 3, but Tally doesn't learn she's a Clone since the Cloning Chamber Fabled character isn't evil.

    How to Run

    Each night, wake Pidgeotto and ask if they want to use their ability. If they say yes, have them point to a player. Mark that player with the Fly 1 token. On the following night, swap it for the Fly 2 token, then the Fly 3 on the following night, removing it at dawn on that third day. As long as Pidgeotto is alive and healthy, the Flying player is safe from evil players' abilities. That means, for example, that James cannot kill them, Jessie registers to them as herself, they cannot be knocked out or killed by Weezing, and no demons can kill them.

    Townsfolk

  11. Alakazam

    Once per game, during the day, privately guess which trainers will faint in the night. That night, if you were correct, you learn the Opponent and their character.

    “065 Psychic Type -Its brain can outperform a supercomputer. Its intelligence quotient is said to be 5,000.”

    Alakazam uses Psychic and learns who the demon is if they can guess exactly which player(s) will die in the night.

    Examples

    • Fran is Alakazam. During the day, she speaks to the Storyteller and guesses that Colton will die in the night. That night Colton and Harris die, so Fran doesn't wake.

    • Brandon is Alakazam. During the day, they speak to the Storyteller and guess that no one will die in the night. That night, the demon chooses Metapod and learns "Metapod used harden." No one dies, and Brandon wakes to learn that Charlie is Giovanni.

    How to Run

    If Alakazam privately guesses which player(s) will die tonight, mark those players with the Psychic token. Each night, if all the players and only all the players marked with the Psychic token died, tell them a demon in play and who they are, then replace the token with the Ability Used token.

    It's valid for Alakazam to guess "no one." In that case, mark a dead player with the Psychic token or set it to the side.

    Townsfolk

  12. Squirtle

    Once per game, during the day, publicly choose one or two other trainers. Pokemon you chose become paralyzed Squirtles that night.

    “007 Water Type - When it retracts its long neck into its shell, it squirts out water with vigorous force.”

    If Squirtle chooses good players to join the Squirtle Squad, those players become Squirtles (with no ability) too.

    Examples

    • Candice is Squirtle. During the day, she publicly claims to be Squirtle and chooses Ben and Jolie. Ben is Persian, so he becomes Squirtle that night and is drunk. Jolie is James, so nothing happens to her at night.

    • Paul is Squirtle. During the day, they publicly claim to be Squirtle and choose Frankie and Simba. Frankie is Onix and Simba is Jessie who chose to register as Machamp at the beginning of the game. Frankie becomes a good Squirtle that night and Simba becomes an evil Squirtle. Both become drunk.

    How to Run

    If Squirtle chooses to use their ability during the day, they choose up to two players. That night, wake any good players they chose and tell them they're Squirtles, then replace their character tokens with a Squirtle token and mark them as drunk.

    Townsfolk

  13. Pikachu

    If you publicly said "Pika" today, tonight, a living neighbor faints.

    “025 Electric Type - Whenever Pikachu comes across something new, it blasts it with a jolt of electricity”

    Pikachu's Thunder can cause players to die in the night by publicly claiming to be Pikachu.

    Examples

    • Jill is Pikachu. Her neighbors are Sally (the demon) and George (Onix). Jill said "Pika" publicly today, so George dies in the night.

    • Connor is Pikachu. His neighbors, Allie and Ben, are dead. Ben's other neighbor is the demon, and Allie's other neighbor, Fran, is a living Meowth. Connor said "Pika" in town publicly today, so Fran dies in the night.

    How to Run

    Each night, if Pikachu said "Pika" while most players and the Storyteller were in Town, mark one of their living neighbors as dead.

    You should usually avoid killing the demon using Pikachu's ability, thus ending the game, but if Wild Pokemon or Giovanni are in play and they've for some reason chosen to not execute a player during the Final Day, it's reasonable to allow good to win.

    Townsfolk

  14. Metapod

    When another trainer’s ability would interact with you, they privately learn that fact, and then it doesn’t.

    “011 Bug Type - Hardens its shell to protect itself. However, a large impact may cause it to pop out of its shell.”

    Metapod uses Harden.

    Examples

    • Robbie is Jynx who chose Metapod on Night 2. They were immediately told Metapod used harden.

    • Alec is Giovanni who chose Joanna on Night 2. He was immediately told Metapod used harden.

    • Ellie is Magneton who is told on Night 4 that Metapod used harden.

    How to Run

    If a player chooses Metapod or their ability would otherwise affect Metapod in any way, ignore that effect on Metapod, and then privately show them the Metapod token as soon as possible.

    Townsfolk

  15. Bulbasaur

    If you faint in the night, you wake and may publicly choose a trainer. The first time this happens, if they’re a Full HP Pokemon, they faint and you’re revived.

    “001 Grass Type - It carries a seed on its back right from birth. As it grows older, the seed also grows larger.”

    Bulbasaur wakes if they would die at night, and they can use Leech Seed to resurrect themselves by choosing a good player to die instead.

    Examples

    • Gordo is Bulbasaur. He's chosen by the demon to die at night, so he wakes and chooses to use his ability. He publicly says, "I claim Bulbasaur and choose Jamie." Jamie is a minion, so the Storyteller marks Gordo as dead.

    • Fabian is Bulbasaur. His neighbor, Linda, is Pikachu, and she said "Pika" today publicly, so one of her living neighbors will die tonight. The Storyteller chooses for Fabian to die, so Fabian wakes and chooses to use his ability. He publicly says, "I'm Bulbasaur and I choose Linda." The Storyteller marks Linda as dead, and Fabian remains alive.

    How to Run

    If Bulbasaur would die at night for any reason, wake them and show them the USE YOUR ABILITY? info token. If they then publicly name a living good player, mark that player dead instead of Bulbasaur. If they choose an evil player, nothing happens.

    Townsfolk

  16. Persian

    Everyone learns Meowth is in play. If you’re mad as Persian, Pikachu might be knocked out.

    “053 "Dark" Type - Although its fur has many admirers, it is tough to raise as a pet because of its fickle meanness. ”

    Persian makes players think Meowth is in play and can get Pikachu executed if they're not careful.

    Examples

    • Lily is Persian. She tells Town that she doesn't believe there's a Meowth in play — it's probably just her ability. Pikachu is executed, and evil wins because there's a Meowth in play.

    • Aoife is Persian and she tells everyone she's Pikachu until the Final Day when she admits in town that she's an outsider, but not which one.

    How to Run

    At the start of Day 1, announce that Meowth is in play. If at any time Persian is 'mad' that they're Persian, from then on, as long as Persian is alive, you can choose to execute Pikachu.

    Persian isn't really Dark type because there are no Dark Pokemon in Gen I, but Alolan Persian is. Don't come for me.

    Outsider

  17. Arbok

    One of your Pokemon neighbors is poisoned. When you die, the other is poisoned from now on.

    “024 Poison Type - It is rumored that the ferocious warning markings on its belly differ from area to area.”

    Arbok Poisons one of the closest Pokemon to them.

    Examples

    • Salem is Arbok sitting between Onix and Pikachu. Onix is poisoned.

    • Pearl is Arbok sitting between Meowth and Persian. Meowth's other neighbor is Dragonite. Dragonite is poisoned.

    How to Run

    During setup, mark one of the closest Pokemon players seated to either side of Arbok as poisoned. Don't forget that outsiders are Pokemon, too!

    Outsider

  18. Snorlax

    When you die, the storyteller chooses two trainers that night: they can't leave town square from now on. If you're knocked out, you might not faint. You're healthy.

    “143 Normal Type - Very lazy. Just eats and sleeps. As its rotund bulk builds, it becomes steadily more slothful.”

    If they're executed, they might not die, but after they do, Snorlax blocks other players from leaving town.

    Examples

    Jerome is Snorlax and is executed on Day 1. They live. On Day 2, they're executed again and die. That night, the Storyteller visits Joe and Alan and tells them they're Blocked and no longer allowed to leave town for private conversations.

    How to Run

    If Snorlax is executed, choose if they die or not. If Snorlax dies, wake two players that night and tell them they're no longer allowed to leave town. Don't confirm for anyone that Snorlax has died, and don't corroborate the two players' story about what happened in the night.

    Outsider

  19. Gengar

    You only have this ability if you’ve fainted: Fainted trainers learn who you are. If your Opponent privately guesses you (once), they wake to use their ability twice that night, even if you're poisoned.

    “094 Ghost Type - Under a full moon, this Pokemon likes to mimic the shadows of people and laugh at their fright.”

    A dead Gengar gives the demon an extra attack if the demon can guess who they are.

    Examples

    Dorothy was killed by the demon on Night 2. Blanche is Meowth. She's executed on Day 2 and learns Dorothy was Gengar. Blanche tells Rose, her demon, so that night she guesses Dorothy and acts twice.

    How to Run

    If Gengar is dead, even if they're poisoned, and the demon ever privately guesses who they are, mark the demon with the Dreameater token. If the demon is marked with the Dreameater token at night, wake them and ask them to use their ability twice then remove the token. Since Wild Pokemon doesn't wake, this ability doesn't affect them.

    Outsider

  20. Jessie

    On your first night, look at the grimoire and choose an out-of-play character. You register as that character, even if dead.",

    “Prepare for trouble...”

    Jessie sees the grimoire and always registers as whatever out-of-play character they chose.

    Examples

    • Mike, Charizard, chooses Emily on Night 3. Emily is Jessie, but she doesn't die because she chose to register as Pikachu.

    • Fran is Jessie and chose to register as Pidgeotto. Bulbasaur dies in the night and publicly chooses Fran. Fran dies and Bulbasaur is resurrected.

    • Alakazam correctly guessed who would die tonight. They wake and learn that Stan is Mewtwo because Stan is Jessie who chose to register as Mewtwo.

    How to Run

    On Night 1, show Jessie the grimoire for as long as they need, then ask them to point to a character that isn't in play. Swap Jessie's token for that character and mark them with the "Is Jessie" token. Except for winning the game, Jessie registers as that character while alive and healthy.

    Minion

  21. James

    Each night choose a player: if they're knocked out today, tonight, choose a trainer: they faint.

    “Make it double...”

    James kills someone if the player they chose got executed during the day.

    Examples

    • James chose Tyson, who is Pikachu, last night, and today Persian was mad that they were Persian. Pikachu is executed and dies. Tonight, James will wake and choose another player to die.

    • James chose Olivia last night, and today she was executed and died. Tonight, James will wake and choose another player to die.

    How to Run

    Each night wake James. If there's a James reminder token on a player that was executed during the previous day, ask them to choose a player to die. Ask them to choose another player, then mark that player with the James reminder token.

    Minion

  22. Meowth

    Everyone knows you’re in play. If Pikachu is knocked out, their team loses. Each night*, you may choose to become Persian.

    “Meeeowth... that's right!”

    Meowth adds Pikachu to the game, and each night either chooses to become Persian so they can try to get Pikachu executed by breaking madness or stays Meowth to try to win by executing Pikachu.

    Examples

    • Blaise is Meowth. On Day 1, the Storyteller announces "Meowth is in play." On Night 3, Blaise chooses to become Persian. On Day 5, when their demon is about to be executed, they announce they're Persian to town. The Storyteller executes Pikachu instead, and goes to Night 6.

    • Courtney is Meowth. On Day 3, Pikachu is nominated and executed. The evil team wins.

    How to Run

    If Pikachu is executed while Meowth is in play, even if they don't then die, announce that the game is over. The evil team wins. Each night after the first, wake Meowth and ask if they want to use their ability. If they say yes, tell them they are now Persian and swap their character token for that one.

    Minion

  23. Weezing

    A Pokemon is poisoned and might faint at any time. When you're knocked out, you may publicly choose a trainer: they faint.

    “WEEEEEE...ZING”

    Weezing makes one Pokemon poisoned and liable to die at any time, and if Weezing is executed, they can kill a player immediately.

    Examples

    • Bastion is Weezing. They're executed on Day 6 and publicly choose to kill a neighbor, leaving only the demon and two other players alive. The demon kills one player in the night, and evil wins.

    • Angela is Weezing, and Jack is poisoned by her ability. Angela asks in town if anyone thinks it might be a Giovanni game, and the Storyteller announces that Jack dies.

    How to Run

    During setup, mark one player as poisoned. Kill them at any time. If Weezing is ever executed and they choose a player, immediately announce that that player dies.

    Minion

  24. Giovanni

    Each night, choose a trainer: before you choose another, they faint. [+Gengar/Arbok/Persian/Snorlax]

    “Well, I certainly won't back down from a challenge if you'd like to battle even more. Heh.”

    Giovanni chooses their kills in advance and can add an arbitrary number of outsiders to the game.

    Examples

    • Last night, Giovanni chose Isaac. Tonight, they choose Slater. The next morning, the Storyteller announces that Isaac died in the night. Later, during nominations, the Storyteller announces that Slater dies.

    How to Run

    During setup, replace any number of townsfolk in the bag with outsiders. Each night, wake Giovanni and have them choose a player. Mark that player with the Challenged token. Sometime before they next choose, mark that player as dead. If it's during the day, announce they've died immediately. If they die at night, don't announce it until the morning.

  25. Elite Four

    Each night*, all Elite Four choose a trainer: one faints. If all Elite Four choose the same, one of them faints instead. [All villains are Elite Four]

    “Lorelei, Bruno, Agatha and Lance”

    The Elite Four requires that good eliminate all evil players to win, but if they aren't coordinated, the Elite Four can accidentally kill their teammates.

    Examples

    • Two Elite Four choose Dana and one chooses Carl. The next day, the Storyteller announces Carl died in the night.

    • The Elite Four all choose Wesley on Night 5. An Elite Four dies.

    How to Run

    During setup, replace all minion tokens with Elite Four. On Night 1, wake the Elite Four and show them the "These are your minions" info token. Each night after the first, wake each of the Elite Four and have them choose a player. Mark each chosen player with a Victory Road reminder token. If all Elite Four chose the same player, mark an Elite Four as dead. Otherwise, mark one of the chosen players as dead.

    Demon

  26. Wild Pokemon

    Each night*, a trainer might die and any Pokemon that incorrectly guessed you today faint. You and your grunts don't learn each other. Each day, if all living good players guess you, your team loses. You can't faint.

    “"chee-CHEE-chew"”

    Wild Pokemon can get multiple kills late in the game, but their minions never know who they are. Luckily, they can only lose if all the good players agree they're the demon.

    Examples

    • Zack is Wild Pokemon. On Day 6, the only living good player guesses Zack is the demon. Evil loses.

    • Tia is Wild Pokemon. On Day 4, their minion, Bentley, publicly guesses that Paul, a good player, is the demon. Paul and the other remaining good player guess that Bentley is the demon. That night, both good players die. Evil wins.

    How to Run

    Skip minion and demon info, but still give Wild Pokemon three bluffs. Each night you can choose to mark any players as dead and then mark any player that publicly guessed that someone else was Wild Pokemon as dead too. If Wild Pokemon is ever executed, announce that they don't die. If all living good players guess them during the day, announce that the game is over and good wins.

    Demon

  27. Mewtwo

    All minions are Clones of one Pokemon, which you know. They don't know you. Each night*, choose a Pokemon: they faint, along with any clones of them. If you chose an out-of-play Pokemon, the storyteller decides who faints. [+? Clones]

    “The circumstances of ones birth are irrelevant. It is what you do with the gift of life that determines who you are.”

    Mewtwo's minions are Clones who don't know they're minions. They choose a Pokemon character to die instead of a player, and if they choose something out-of-play, the Storyteller chooses the deaths.

    Examples

    • Mewtwo chooses Squirtle. Three players are Squirtle, but only one of them dies.

    • Mewtwo chooses Onix. There's no Onix in play, so one of the minions dies and learns they're an evil Clone.

    • On Night 2, Mewtwo chooses Machamp. Their Clones are clones of Machamp, so that night, three players die.

    How to Run

    During setup, choose a Pokemon for the minions to be clones of. Replace the minion tokens with copies of that token. Choose the appropriate number of players that drew that token to be Clones. Skip Minion info, but during Demon info, point to the Clone's character on the script after showing them to the demon. Each night after the first, wake Mewtwo and have them point to a character on the script. If that character is in play, mark them and any Clones of them as dead.

    It's valid to choose to make other trainers Clones of the same or even different in-play Pokemon, but they're not minions, and they're still good.

    Demon

  28. Cloning Chamber

    Trainers can be Clones. Clones believe they're an in-play Pokemon and might register as one, but they're not. Clones learn their alignment and that they're clones when they faint.

    “"I'll decide my own rules. . ."”

    Mewtwo's clones think they're good Pokemon, but they're evil minions. They only learn that they're evil Clones if they die.

    Examples

    Mewtwo is in play in a 12-player game, and three of the players have the Charizard token, but two are evil Clones. Two other players have the Bulbasaur token, but one is a good Clone.

    How to Run

    Add Clones to the game if Mewtwo is the demon.

    Fabled

  29. Mew

    Each day, if a trainer was knocked out, their team loses if a trainer of the opposing team fainted last night.

    “Mew, mew mew mew, mew mew.”

    Mew's Mysterious Power makes the day's executee lose if a player of the opposite alignment died in the night.

    Examples

    Alice is Mew. Scott is evil and is executed on Day 2. Good wins because a good player was killed by the demon the previous night.

    How to Run

    Each night, add Mew's reminder token to the player(s) who died. If a player of the opposing alignment is executed the following day, announce that the game is over and that player's team loses.

    Traveler

  30. Melmetal

    If there's ever been a Meltan in play, you cannot be exiled. You might register as a Pokemon on the final day.

    “MELMETAAAL”

    Melmetal can't be exiled if they evolved from Meltan (or if there has ever been another player who is Meltan in the game), and on the final day, they might keep the game going for good.

    How to Run

    If Meltan was ever in play, don't accept calls for Melmetal's exile. On the final day, choose to register Melmetal as a Pokemon or not. If Melmetal is a Pokemon, the good team doesn't lose if the demon is alive with Melmetal and one other player.

    Traveler

  31. Meltan

    On your third night, you evolve into Melmetal.

    “mel tan”

    Meltan can become Melmetal by evolving.

    How to Run

    Each night, mark Meltan with the appropriate night reminder token. On their third night, wake Melmetal and show them the Melmetal token, then swap their token for it.

    There can be more than one Melmetal in play after Meltan evolves.
